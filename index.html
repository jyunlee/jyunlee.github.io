Jihyun Lee

Postdoctoral AI Research Scientist at Meta

Hello! 👋  I am a Postdoctoral AI Research Scientist at Meta (Codec Avatars Lab), where I am fortunate to work with Shunsuke Saito and Sofien Bouaziz. Previously, I obtained my Ph.D. at KAIST advised by Tae-Kyun (T-K) Kim and Minhyuk Sung. I visited Meta Reality Labs as a research scientist intern in 2024, hosted by Jason Saragih.

My research aims to enable artificial intelligence to understand and simulate human behavior, including interactions with the surrounding environment. Recently, I have been especially interested in human-centric world models.
